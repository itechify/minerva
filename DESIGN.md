# Design System: Minerva

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Window the Game Forgot to Ship"**

The overlay borrows the Mini Map's grey window bar, bright blue buttons and sky-blue title panel, and the Quest
Helper's see-through periwinkle body. The Map Mark sits beside Street Name over Map Name in light, blue-outlined
lettering. Minimap glyphs carry the map's meaning; the pixel-art owl marks the window. Dense, near-black text is read
in glances between attacks. Setup and the site use opaque lavender, navy ink and steel buttons, at a reading size.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Game-window chrome, small corners and bevels, without drop shadows.
- Orange for doubt and problems; the minimap's colours keep their meanings.
- Maplestory Light and Bold throughout, with numeric columns right-aligned.
- Pointer, pressed and keyboard-focus states on controls; motion answers the player, never a timer.

Tokens in the frontmatter are normative. The sections below explain their application. Surface composition lives in
Components; shared sizing and responsive rules live in Layout. Terms belong to `GLOSSARY.md`, player instructions and
literal copy to `site/faq/index.html`, and behaviour and rationale to `docs/adr/` (references beside each surface).

## Colors

### Primary
- **Portal Ring Blue:** portals to another map, Doors included, and the Map section's legend ring on Portal Ring Fill.

### Secondary
- **Game Orange:** uncertainty and problems, paired with words and a chip, dot or bordered note. Doubt Gold to Doubt
  Amber draws the static doubt chip. Problem Cream, Amber and Brown carry notices and states to notice, including
  paused, refused keys and an armed confirmation; those states use no orange unless there is a problem.

### Tertiary
- **Mob Dot Red:** mobs and their Spawn Points, with Mob Edge.
- **Party Gold / Party Ink:** the two-figure party mark and small Party labels; the darker ink holds on lavender.
- **You Yellow:** the player's location and their best, lit by You Light and edged by You Edge. Use a dot for location
  and a star for a best; never a chip or note.
- **Crown Gold, Silver and Bronze:** top-three rank only, in a 14px crown, never a fill, chip or text. Light the metals
  from `#ffe58a` to `#d99a00`, `#f4f6fa` to `#7e8898`, and `#f7d6b6` to `#a5602b`, with their crown-edge tokens.
- **Teleport Green:** a portal within the map and its dashed arrow. **NPC Green:** a filled NPC dot inside its Route
  Magenta ring, brighter and yellower than the teleport's ring.
- **Unknown Grey:** a portal to a map absent from the data.
- **Route Magenta:** the way to a Hunting Target or Destination, drawn as a wide ring, never a fill, tag or text.
- **EXP Lime:** EXP readings and EXP/h lanes only, on EXP Track grey with EXP Frame. Lost ground, notches and rulers
  use frame grey, never lime.

### Neutral
- **Quest Helper Glass:** the see-through ground of windows over the game; over white, the site's page ground.
- **Window Bar Grey / Window Bar Ink:** window chrome and its names; the shared `--window-bar` gradient.
- **Mini Map Sky / Title Lettering:** the map's title panel, lettering outlined in Title Outline.
- **Window Edge / Panel Rule / Window Rule:** frames and internal hairlines, not separators around every section.
- **Panel Ink, Steel and Slate:** primary, secondary and tertiary text on glass. **Ink, Steel and Slate:** the navy
  equivalents on opaque lavender. The collapsed icon's badges use navy grounds with white text.
- **Window Lavender / Window Lavender, Opaque:** translucent inset surfaces (`--body`, including the Grind Tracker's
  figures) and opaque desktop/site bodies (`--body-solid`). The site's tab band, credits and feature stages mix the
  opaque lavender 12% toward Frame Navy. Frame Navy edges secondary buttons and fields.
- **Inset / Inset Edge:** a pale list or box inside the body. Dense figures and tables use near-opaque Window Lavender
  with the same edge. Results Header keeps scrolled rows from showing through sticky headers.
- **Selection Periwinkle / Selection Edge:** the selected row or band, paired with a non-colour cue. Selection Edge
  also draws scrollbar thumbs.
- **Tag Periwinkle:** ordinary descriptive tags. **Quest Violet:** Quest tags only. Tags never use Teleport Green.
- **Edit Blue:** keyboard focus and checked controls; at 8%, the row-hover tint. It also draws the hero film's dashed
  title box and read line.
- **Minimap Paper:** the pale, see-through paper under map pictures; retain white halos on marks over the game.
- **Game Button Blue:** window-bar controls. **Button Steel / Light Steel:** primary and secondary controls; hover and
  pressed gradients shift one step. **Greyed:** disabled controls, flat without highlights.
- **Navy Veil:** the page behind the site's Report window.
- **Minimap Night, Grass, Moss, Earth and Rope:** the site minimap's art. The site's foot repeats its ground as a 14px
  strip, grass (2px) over moss (2px) over earth.

### Named Rules
**The Orange Means Doubt Rule.** Game orange marks uncertainty and problems, never a confirmed state or search tier.

**The Minimap Legend Rule.** Portal blue, mob red, NPC green and you yellow keep their minimap meanings. Yellow also
marks the player's best. Teleport Green, Unknown Grey and Route Magenta each keep the single meaning above.

**The EXP Lime Rule.** Lime draws EXP, never generic progress or rank. A linked Character's lane stays lime like
everyone else's; their star identifies them.

**The Item Grade Rule.** Deal Badges use Button Steel, Epic Purple and Unique Gold for increasing deal grades
(ADR 0014). Gold has dark ink and a bright top shine; the doubt chip has amber and white lettering.

**The Over the Game, See-Through Rule.** Windows over the game use glass; desktop windows use opaque lavender.

**The Two Inks Rule.** Glass uses near-black Panel Ink, lavender navy Ink. Set the panel's own colour explicitly so
figures cannot inherit the body's navy.

## Typography

Maplestory is the only family, bundled in `assets/fonts` (licence in NOTICE), with the fallback stack in the tokens.
`src/fonts.css` maps weights through 500 to Light, 600 and up to Bold. Weight and ink carry hierarchy. The app uses
`font-display: block` and remeasures once loaded; the site serves WOFF2 with `swap`. Missing dashes and the minus sign
fall back to Arial. On glass a 0.3px stroke in the text's own colour strengthens Light without clogging Bold; outlined
titles and the doubt chip take none.

### Hierarchy
- **Headline:** Map Name, balanced and wrapping to at most two lines. With no map the slot uses Status in plain Panel
  Ink without an outline. Time to Level and setup step headings also use plain ink. Setup's EXP test is 16px/22px bold.
- **Street:** Street Name above Map Name, sharing its outlined lettering.
- **Section Heading:** Map, Navigation, Mobs and the Hunting Target's mob, one step above bold rows.
- **Window Name:** names and search titles on the bar; a picked mob's level is regular. Setup's name is 14px, its step
  count uses Window Name.
- **Title:** portal destinations, summary and rate values, target figures and arrival status.
- **Body Reading:** setup prose and search input. Setup notices, found map and shortcuts use Body with an 18px line.
- **Body:** notes, mob and rates tables, suggestions and otherwise unassigned panel text. Mob names are bold.
- **Body Small:** labels, units, headers, routes, candidates, hints and credits; line-height 15px for one line, 16px
  wrapping, 17px in table rows. Candidate IDs are 11px regular.
- **Button:** sentence case. Setup's Overlay Guide button is 12px.
- **Label:** sentence-case tags, untracked. Only the doubt chip is uppercase, tracked 0.04em at a 13px line.

Warming EXP figures drop to Light; Best and Loot stay bold. Paused and unreadable figures stay bold, explained by a tag
or note. Use weight rather than opacity so readings stay legible. A missing figure's reason is Body Panel Ink Steel.
The collapsed icon's count (10px/13px bold) and Time to Level (9px/12px bold, Light on Ink Steel while held) are
exceptions, not scale steps.

The site uses the reading roles in the frontmatter. Under 640px: Body 15px/24px, Title 32px/38px, Opener 26px/32px,
Topic 22px/28px and Lede 16px/26px. The first feature is a size up: 36px/42px, words 19px/31px; on phones 30px/36px
and 17px/28px. Topic intros use 15px/23px Ink Steel. Paragraphs, lists and definition lists have 20px gaps (18px on
phones, 12px in answers); list items 8px (4px in answers), ring-key fragments 3px. Subheads have 32px above and 10px

### Motion

**The Motion Answers Rule.** Motion shows where something the player reached for comes from. What the game changes
changes in place, at once or with a cross-fade of at most 150ms. EXP gauge, Pace Trace and forecast never ease.

Use one authored motion per window, 150–400ms on exponential ease-out: `1 − 2^(−10t)` scaled to land exactly in script,
`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` in CSS. First frames are readable. Zoom interpolates in log scale while content keeps
its size. No loops, pulses, shimmer, timers, bounce or overshoot; doubt chip and alert dot hold still. With
`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, show the final state immediately. Mark Card's dive and Deal Badges' unfold are
specified with those components; the site's hero and minimap follow scroll, as do Pedestals' rising blocks.

- **Band morph:** same-document View Transition, 300ms. Shared Characters slide between ranks (the linked Character
  below the top 50 too); other rows cross-fade and lanes regrow from the left. Animate only transform and opacity,
  sticky head above rows, new band readable immediately. Arrivals and deep links open still, scrolled to the linked
  entry. No View Transitions, reduced motion or browser-drawn swipe-back switches at once; without script, plain links.
- **Pedestal restack:** 300ms transform and opacity via Web Animations. Shared Characters and their details move
  together to new steps; blocks hold still. Crossing Characters fade as they pass, new Characters fade in. Both podiums
  occupy one measured box, the hidden one out of sight, so surrounding content stays put. Reduced motion is immediate.
- **Podium rise:** CSS view timeline from 30% to 100% visible. Blocks and Characters rise from a clipping floor to
  their own heights, first place furthest; scrolling back reverses it. If already in view on arrival, no view timelines
  or reduced motion, open fully stood.
- **Character seam:** a name clicked on the boards glides into the Character page's Site Title in 320ms, while the
  page cross-fades in 200ms and the sticky head settles. Cross-document View Transition (`site/seams.html`), transform
  and opacity only; heading width fits its words while scaling. Only pre-paint content participates, never API-filled
  content. Other links, Back/Forward, unsupported browsers and reduced motion use plain navigation.

## Elevation & Depth

No window has a drop shadow. Depth comes from the game's window chrome: a faint white ring just inside the frame, bevels
on the buttons (a white top highlight, and on the game's blue buttons a darker bottom edge), 1px ring strokes around
glyphs, and the outlined lettering of a map's title. The panel sits flat on the game scene, and its see-through glass is
what joins it to the game's windows. A pressed button sinks with an inset shadow rather than lifting; the search field
is the one surface set into the body, with a soft inner shadow. While the map shown isn't confirmed, the facts recede to
70% opacity rather than moving.

**The Chrome, Not Lift Rule.** Depth is drawn the way the game draws it, with highlights, bevels and hairlines. Nothing

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** reuse the materials, semantic colours and type roles above; keep component exceptions local.
- **Do** pair colour states with a shape and words, and give controls hover, pressed and keyboard-focus states.
- **Do** keep figures right-aligned, notes wrapping and single-line facts truncating.
- **Do** honour reduced motion and keep a motion's first frame readable.
- **Do** draw minimap glyphs and line controls in CSS or inline SVG; use the game's art for Map Marks and job icons,
  pixel art for the owl and site illustrations. Keep screenshots as figures, not icons.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use orange, or the outlined title lettering, for anything other than their single roles.
- **Don't** add small labels or kickers above content beyond the Street Name in the title panel; a label belongs only to
  the field or figure it names.
- **Don't** let a click on the overlay take the keyboard from the game outside the search view, nor one on the Settings
  window outside a Change and the Deal Margin field.
- **Don't** animate arrivals or decorate changing game readings with motion; follow Layout's motion rules.
- **Don't** float any window on a blurred drop shadow; depth is highlights, bevels and hairlines.
- **Don't** introduce a second typeface or icon fonts.
