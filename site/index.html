The map you're on, at a glance

A free overlay for MapleStory Classic World that shows the map you're on: every portal, even the hidden ones, its mobs, and the way to the mob you want to hunt.

It only looks at the screen, the way a screenshot does: it never reads the game's memory or presses keys for you.

Every portal, even the hidden ones

The Map Preview shows the whole map you're on with every portal ringed, and lists the Hidden Portals the game doesn't show, with where each one leads.

What a map is worth

The Map Summary gives the EXP and mobs per hour you can expect there, solo or in a party, then lists every mob by level. Point at one to light its spawn points on the map.

The way to any mob or map

Search a mob and Minerva lists the maps it spawns on, best first. Pick one as your Hunting Target and it shows the way there, portal by portal.

Your pace, and your best on every map

Minerva reads your EXP bar by itself, and the Grind Tracker shows your EXP per hour and time to level, races your last 5 minutes against the ones before, and keeps each character's Personal Best on every map.

What others get on every map

The Map Leaderboards put players' Personal Bests from Minerva side by side on each map, every Level Band ranked by EXP per hour, so you can see what players at your level get there. A new character's bests join them as you play, shared by default: Share, under their name in your Personal Bests, turns it off.

What an item is worth

Point at an item and press Ctrl+Shift+I. Its Item Card shows what a shop pays for it, beside the market prices you keep.

Any item's page in one key

Point at an item and press Ctrl+Shift+U. Item Lookup opens its MeowDB page: where it drops, and the quests that give or need it.

The deals in any Player Shop

Open a Player Shop and press Ctrl+Shift+D. The Deal Finder marks each listing priced below the market price you keep for it, until you press it again.

How it stays safe

Minerva is a third-party tool, not made or approved by Nexon. Nexon's terms forbid some third-party programs, and a penalty in Classic World applies to your whole account, MapleStory included. Using Minerva is at your own risk.

To keep that risk low, Minerva only looks at the screen:

  • It captures the minimap title and the bar at the bottom of the game (and, if you choose, an item's tooltip or a Player Shop) the same way a screenshot does, and draws its own window.
  • It never reads or changes the game's memory or files, and never injects anything into it.
  • It never presses keys or clicks for you, and automates nothing.

Download for Windows

  1. Download and run Minerva. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Keep the minimap showing. Open or collapsed, anywhere in the game window: Minerva finds its title by itself. On a map without a minimap, it reads the bar the game shows instead.
  3. Play. The panel shows beside the game, and follows you from map to map.
  4. Update when you like. Minerva says when a new version is out, and installs it when you choose Update.

Hotkeys

They work while the game or one of Minerva's windows is in front, and the game never sees them. Change any of them in Settings, the gear at the top of the panel.
