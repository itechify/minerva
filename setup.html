Minerva Setup

Before you start: your account

Minerva is a third-party tool, not made or approved by Nexon. Nexon's terms forbid some third-party programs, and a penalty in Classic World applies to your whole account, MapleStory included. Using Minerva is at your own risk.

To keep that risk low, Minerva only looks at the screen:

  • It captures the minimap title and the bar at the bottom of the game (and, if you choose, an item's tooltip or a Player Shop) the same way a screenshot does, and draws its own window.
  • It never reads or changes the game's memory or files, and never injects anything into it.
  • It never presses keys or clicks for you, and automates nothing.

Find the game window

Set up the game

  • Keep the minimap showing, open or collapsed. Minerva reads the street name and map name in its title, wherever you put it.
  • Play in windowed or borderless mode. Screen capture can't see exclusive fullscreen, and the panel can only dock beside a window.

Finding your map

There's nothing to mark: Minerva finds the minimap's title by itself, wherever you put the minimap, open or collapsed. On a map without a minimap, it reads the bar the game shows there instead.

Grind Tracker

The Grind Tracker shows how fast you level. There's nothing to mark: Minerva reads the EXP figures on the EXP bar at the bottom of the game, like EXP. 160 [3.81%], by itself.

Help & Setup

Drag the panel by its header to move it, Inside or Detached; Docked panels stay beside the game. Placement cycles through Inside, Docked Right, Docked Left, and Detached. The magnifier at the top of the panel, or the search shortcut, finds mobs, maps and items. Pick a mob, then one of the maps it spawns on, to make it your Hunting Target, or pick a map to make it your Destination: the panel shows the way there until you arrive. To see what an item is worth, pick it in search, or hover it in the game until its tooltip shows and press the item card shortcut: its card shows what a shop pays for it, and the market price you keep for it, with a button to look it up on MesoWatch. Its name opens its MeowDB page in your browser, as the MeowDB shortcut does at once.

Map Summary: 6+ means a party of six or more players. Share is a mob's share of all spawns on the Map.

Something wrong? Choose Bug Report in the menu of Minerva's owl in the notification area, by the clock. It saves what Minerva read and made of it, and opens a form to report the bug on GitHub with it.
