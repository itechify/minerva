Hotkeys

While the game or Minerva is in front, these keys act here and never reach the game.

    Market Prices

    Item Cards show the Going rate and Cheapest now from maplestory.market, and the Deal Finder can judge against them. Minerva asks owlofminerva.app for the items you look at, sending only their item numbers. Off, it asks for none.

    Deal Finder

    A listing is a deal when one item costs at least the Deal Margin less than its price on the Deal Line.

    Grind Tracker

    Reads your EXP from the game's Status Bar, by itself, to show how fast you level. Off, Minerva doesn't read your EXP at all.

    Game Data

    Maps, mobs and items come from OSMS Data Explorer. Minerva gets them again once a day, and when it's updated. After a patch, Refresh gets them now.

    Updates

    Minerva checks GitHub for a new version when it starts and every 6 hours, sending only its version. It never updates without you. Off, it checks only when you press Check for updates in Help & Setup.
